About Me

Munavath Sriramulu (also known as Munavath Sriramulu Nayak) is a Hyderabad-based linguist, educator, AI data operations professional, and social activist from Telangana.

Career & Professional Roles

AI & Multilingual Operations: He works as an Operations Supervisor and Multilingual AI/OCR Evaluation Lead at TP (Teleperformance), specializing in NLP workflows, data validation, and retail receipt transcription for machine intelligence projects.

Education & Language Training: He founded Munavath Sriramulu Edu-Tech Consultancy in Saroornagar, Hyderabad, which offers courses in European and Nordic languages — including German, Danish, Swedish, and Dutch — as well as translation services. He previously served as a faculty member in the Department of English at Osmania University.

Tourism & Translation: Licensed by the Government of India, he works as a multilingual tour guide and interpreter (speaking German, Russian, Spanish, Hindi, Telugu, and English). He also served as a liaison officer and interpreter during the 2013 UNWTO Conference on Sustainable Tourism Development in Hyderabad.

Education

Linguistics & Humanities: Integrated Master's degree in German from English and Foreign Languages University (EFLU), MA in Russian from IGNOU, and MA in Sociology from Osmania University.

Technology, Finance & Sciences: Additional postgraduate qualifications from Osmania University include MCA, MBA in Finance, and MSc in Applied Geochemistry.

Social Activism & Non-Profit Work

Student Advocacy: During his time as an Adivasi research scholar at EFLU in the early 2010s, he gained public attention for campaigning against alleged caste-based discrimination on campus.

Non-Profit Initiatives: He is associated with educational and social welfare organizations, including the Nalgonda-based Munavath Sriramulu International Research Foundation (registered in 2024) and The Help and Peace Society.

Public & Political Engagement: On social media, he campaigns under the name Munavath Sriramulu Nayak, focusing on Gormati/Banjara community outreach and expressing political aspirations for the Nalgonda Lok Sabha constituency.

Mission: From India to Abrahamic Family House — Fostering Cooperation, Building Bridges, Strengthening Peace.
